AdRoll HTML5 banners

Four variations, two audiences. Each variation has six sizes. Open a variation to play the ads and download the upload zips.

Segment 1 — High-intent visitors

Membership page visitors. The goal is to get past hesitation about cost, commitment, and care levels, and book a discovery call. Landing page: the Five Journeys membership page.

Variation 1A — Social proof and insurance

0–4s seated photo of Dr. Wendie and Dr. Ed. 4–8s reviews and appointments. 8–12s insurance line and a Schedule Your Call button.

Variation 1B — Tiered care and personalized access

0–4s a provider with a patient. 4–8s four membership tiers. 8–12s a Compare Memberships or Schedule Your Call button.

Segment 2 — Mid-intent visitors

People who viewed two or more pages. The goal is to answer root-cause frustration and offer a low-friction next step. Landing page: the homepage, the health quiz, or the discovery-call page.

Variation 2A — Normal labs and root-cause relief

0–4s a lifestyle photo and the “labs are normal” line. 4–8s comprehensive testing. 8–12s a Schedule Your Call button.

Variation 2B — Health quiz

0–4s Dr. Wendie and Dr. Ed. 4–8s hidden triggers. 8–12s a Take the Health Quiz button.
