Bookings follow Bruno’s discovery-call status rules (Nov 2, 2026). A qualified booking is a lead’s first valid discovery-call booking (not cancelled, invalid or a no-show) with First Appointment Date set. A call is held (assumed) if it is marked showed, or is still booked or confirmed and its time has passed; a future call is upcoming; a reschedule is judged at its new time. Counts go by booking month and first-touch source (organic Google or other organic search), all locations. Historic counts are a floor: in Aug–Oct 2026, 41% of all discovery-call bookings showed as referrals from fivejourneys.com and 39% as direct or unknown, because the booking iframe hid the true source. Nonbrand and share rows use the Search Console query table, which leaves out anonymized queries (named queries are about 43% of all clicks); the brand filter covers Five Journeys spellings and clinician names from the site’s team page.

Data as of: GoHighLevel bookings through Nov 2, 2026 (last loaded Nov 2, 1:40 PM ET), so Oct 2026 is complete. GA4 and Search Console as above (Sep 2026 latest complete month).

Sources and method

All figures come from Drawer A (Postgres) as of Nov 3, 2026. Monthly KPIs use 2026 monthly averages over complete months: Jan–Sep 2026 for GA4, Search Console, Business Profile and Ahrefs, and Jan–Oct or Aug–Oct 2026 for GoHighLevel bookings. Snapshot KPIs (Ahrefs keyword counts, BrightLocal ranks) show the first 2026 value and the latest. KPI names and targets come from the SEO Sally job page. GA4 and Search Console are the record; Ahrefs and BrightLocal are tool estimates, used as early signals. This page shows totals only: no patient, lead or contact details.
