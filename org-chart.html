Two views of Five Journeys marketing. The current-state chart was approved Nov 24, 2026. The vision chart is the intended structure, not a claim that every seat is already live. People are shown by first name. Bots are named as they are known in the department.

Current state (approved Nov 24, 2026)

Bruno (CMO, Five Journeys) sits at the top. Mark-stro (Chief of Staff) and QA Quinton (QA) report to Bruno. Gateway Grace (CTO) and the CMO Bot (not live yet) report to Mark-stro. SEO Sally reports to the CMO Bot, and to Mark-stro until the CMO Bot is live.

Legend

  • A solid line means “reports to.”
  • A dotted line means “works with.” It is not a second reporting line.
  • Bots support people and never manage them.
  • Nothing goes out without a person’s or Bruno’s yes.
  • There are no dotted lines among Grace, Sally, and Quinton. Their link remains the site-change workflow: Sally finds, Grace builds on staging, Quinton tests, Bruno approves.
